Voice Agent

          
Deploy a live voice agent to a phone number in a single API call.

          
POST/v1/agents/deploy

          
Deploy an agent to a phone number

          
voice agent · deploy
curl -X POST https://api.squadron.tel/v1/agents/deploy \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sq_live_..." \
  -d '{ "agent":"alex","phone":"+15550001234" }'

# Response
{"agent_id":"agt_9xKmPq2r","status":"live"}

FAQ

    
Still have
questions?

    

      

        Can customers tell it's AI? +
        
Most can't. Our agents are trained on natural speech patterns with realistic pacing, filler words, and personality. In our internal testing, fewer than 8% of callers ask if they're speaking with a bot — and when they do, the agent answers honestly and offers to transfer to a human if needed.

      

      

        What if the agent doesn't know the answer? +
        
The agent escalates. You define escalation rules in the dashboard — by topic, intent, or sentiment. When something falls outside the knowledge base, the call transfers to a live rep with full context already in the handoff note. No customer left hanging.

      

      

        How long does training take? +
        
You can paste in FAQs, upload PDFs, or link your helpdesk in about five minutes. The agent is trained on that content instantly. For large knowledge bases (1,000+ articles), a full sync takes under an hour. No engineering required at any step.

      

      

        What phone system do you integrate with? +
        
Squadron is powered by Twilio under the hood, so it works with your existing phone setup via SIP forwarding, or you can use one of our provisioned numbers. We also integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, and Zapier out of the box.

      

      

        Is there a free trial? +
        
The Scout plan ships with 250 free minutes — enough to handle real calls and see the quality for yourself. No credit card required to start. You'll hear the difference in the first call.

      

      

        What's the latency like on voice calls? +
        
Response latency averages 400ms from end of speech to first word of reply — indistinguishable from a normal conversational pause. We run on edge infrastructure close to your callers. There's no perceptible AI delay.

      

      

        How is pricing calculated? +
        
Monthly flat rate per plan, plus usage-based overage on voice minutes at $0.04/min if you exceed your plan's included minutes. Chat conversations are unlimited on Commander and above. No per-seat fees, no per-agent fees.
