FAQ

Still have
questions?

Does the agent say it is an AI? +

Yes. Every Squadron agent identifies itself as an AI at the start of every conversation, and every call begins with an audible recording notice. Squadron answers inbound conversations only; it never places outbound AI calls.

What if the agent doesn't know the answer? +

It says so, and it escalates. Every agent answers only from your Business Profile, and when a question falls outside it the agent tells the customer it does not have that information, takes a message or transfers to your on-call contact, and logs the gap in Squadron HQ so you can approve an answer for next time.

What does Squadron need from me? +

A website URL, an iOS App Store URL, or your documents. Squadron builds a Business Profile from them, shows every field with its source, and asks you to correct anything before your team goes live. Nothing is invented.

Do I need a new phone number? +

Squadron gives you a phone number. You can publish it directly or forward your existing business line to it from your current carrier.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day trial that includes a real phone number, and no card is required to start.

How is pricing calculated? +

A flat monthly rate per plan with a stated voice-minute and conversation allowance. When you reach the allowance, Squadron pauses and notifies you; you can add a one-click pack. There is no automatic upgrade and no per-agent fee.
