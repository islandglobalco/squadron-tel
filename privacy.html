Privacy Policy

Squadron (squadron.tel) · Effective October 3, 2026

1. Who We Are; Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Island Global Co DBA Squadron, a Delaware company ("we," "us"), collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information in connection with Squadron and squadron.tel (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this Policy and to our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, do not use the Service. The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Information you give us: name, email address, phone number, account credentials, billing details, messages, prompts, questions, files, photos, survey answers, support requests and any other content you submit. Payment card data is collected by our payment processors, not stored by us.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, device and browser identifiers, operating system, language, referring pages, pages and features used, timestamps, approximate location derived from IP, crash and performance data, and interaction data, using cookies, pixels, SDKs, server logs and similar technologies.
  • Information from third parties: sign-in providers (such as Apple or Google), payment processors, affiliate networks, data vendors, public records and sources, and analytics and security providers.
  • Inferences and AI-generated data: classifications, summaries and outputs created from your inputs.

Do not give us sensitive information (such as Social Security, government ID or financial account numbers, health information, or privileged materials) unless the Service specifically asks for it. If you do, you consent to our processing it as described here.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to: provide, operate, maintain and secure the Service; create and manage accounts; process payments and orders; generate outputs and personalize results; communicate with you, including service messages and, where permitted, marketing; analyze and improve the Service; detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and illegal activity; enforce our Terms and protect our rights, users and the public; comply with law and legal process; and for any other purpose you consent to. We may create aggregated or de-identified data, which we may use and disclose for any purpose.

Squadron does not sell personal information, does not share it for advertising, and does not use callers' conversations to train AI models.

4. How We Disclose Information

We disclose information to: service providers and processors that act for us (such as cloud hosting and infrastructure, AI model providers, email and SMS delivery, payment and banking, analytics, customer support and security vendors); affiliates; advertising and affiliate partners (limited identifiers and click data); professional advisers; authorities, courts and counterparties where we believe disclosure is required or appropriate to comply with law or protect rights, safety or property; in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, bankruptcy or sale of assets; and with your consent or at your direction. Content you submit to AI features is transmitted to our third-party AI providers to produce outputs; those providers process it under their own terms and our agreements with them.

5. No Sale of Personal Information; Cookies and Advertising

We do not sell personal information for money. Some cookies, affiliate links and advertising or analytics tools may disclose identifiers or browsing activity to third parties, which certain state laws call "sharing" or "targeted advertising." You may opt out by emailing us, by using your browser's cookie controls, or by enabling Global Privacy Control, which we honor where required. We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals except as required by law. Blocking cookies may break parts of the Service.

Squadron uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser remembers your setup progress while you build your team. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on squadron.tel.

6. Artificial Intelligence, Service Providers and Training

Squadron uses third-party AI services to generate agent answers, voices and business profiles. Customer content and End User conversations are sent to those providers to produce outputs. We do not use End Users' conversations or Customer content to train generative AI models, and we do not authorize our AI providers to do so; under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models by default. We cannot guarantee any provider's practices, which are governed by its own terms and by our agreements with it. We may use de-identified, aggregated operational metrics to run, secure and improve Squadron.

We use these categories of service providers, each of which receives only what it needs: OpenAI (agent answers, voice and profiles), Twilio (phone calls and recordings), Cloudflare (call bridge), Vercel (hosting, file and recording storage), Neon (database), Resend (email), Stripe (card payments) and Mercury (bank wires). They may process data in the United States and other countries.

7. Callers and Chat Visitors; Our Role as Service Provider

When you call or chat with a business that uses Squadron, we process your phone number, call time and length, an audio recording, a transcript, the messages you send, and any name, number or request you leave. Every call begins with a notice that it is answered by an AI and recorded. We process this information on behalf of that business, which decides how it is used, shared and kept and which is the right first contact for requests about it. If you do not want to be recorded, hang up. We will help the business respond to requests we receive for it, and we may pass your request along to it.

8. Retention and Deletion

We keep business content, conversations and recordings while the account is open so the business can review them. A business account owner can delete the account at any time from the account page. Deletion is immediate: the account, its teams, profiles, conversations, transcripts and call recordings are erased at that moment, subject to short-lived backup and log rotation, and we keep only paid invoice and transaction records (without the email address) where tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or other law or legitimate legal needs require. A business may also ask us to delete a specific conversation or recording.

You use the Service and transmit information at your own risk. You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential. To the fullest extent permitted by law, we are not liable for unauthorized access, loss or disclosure that results from circumstances outside our reasonable control.
